# visionAid
### Seeing Possibilities. Navigating Life with Confidence.

**visionAid** is an assistive-technology project under development by **Oversight Innovative Technologies (OIT)**, created with a mission to help blind and low-vision people navigate everyday life with greater independence, awareness, and confidence.

By exploring the potential of artificial intelligence, computer vision, and accessible mobile technology, visionAid aims to make useful environmental information more accessible to people who experience visual impairment.

> Technology should not only help us see what is possible; it should help everyone experience more of what life has to offer.

## About the Project

Development of visionAid began around June 2025, and the project remains under active development.

The current minimum viable product (MVP) is being developed and tested using a mobile phone. This stage provides an opportunity to build, evaluate, and refine the experience before exploring future hardware integrations.

The long-term vision extends beyond mobile devices. I intend to explore integrating visionAid with AI-powered smart glasses, with the goal of providing more natural, hands-free assistance that can fit into users' everyday routines.

## The Vision

visionAid is being developed with the goal of helping users access useful information about their surroundings and everyday activities.

Planned and intended capabilities include:

- **Object and obstacle awareness:** Helping users identify objects and potential obstacles in their surroundings.
- **Road-sign recognition:** Exploring ways to recognize road signs and communicate relevant information to users.
- **Currency recognition:** Helping users identify banknotes and understand their denominations.
- **Everyday assistance:** Supporting users as they navigate daily routines and interact with their environment.
- **AI-powered smart glasses:** Exploring future integration with compatible wearable hardware to support hands-free interaction.

These capabilities represent the project's intended direction and are subject to development, testing, hardware compatibility, and accessibility evaluation.

## Current Development Stage

**Status: MVP development and mobile testing**

The project is currently being developed independently by its founder.

Current priorities include developing the mobile experience, evaluating the feasibility of intended capabilities, refining the user experience, and exploring the technical requirements for future wearable integration.

The project has not yet established that every planned capability is production-ready. Reliability, accessibility, and real-world safety will be important considerations throughout development.

visionAid is intended to complement, not replace, established mobility aids, orientation and mobility training, or other appropriate accessibility support. Any future navigation-related features will require careful testing before being relied upon in safety-critical situations.

## Future Direction

The long-term ambition is to explore visionAid as an intelligent assistive experience that can move beyond the smartphone and potentially operate through AI-powered glasses.

This direction may create opportunities to investigate:

- Hands-free, context-aware assistance.
- Real-time environmental information.
- More natural voice-based interactions.
- Integration with compatible wearable devices.
- Accessible experiences shaped by feedback from blind and low-vision users.

These are future objectives, not claims of existing hardware compatibility or completed functionality.
